The term is “on-us.” It’s when the same bank is on both sides of a transaction, issuing the customer’s card and acquiring for the merchant.

Yes, banks can process on-us card transactions internally without sending them through Visa’s switch. The bank approves the payment and moves the money inside its own books.

There are two catches. Visa’s rules still apply to a Visa-branded card, and a smaller bank may use an outside card processor.

The cleaner option skips the card: the customer pays from a checking account at the same bank. It works when both parties bank there and needs integration with the bank’s core system.
