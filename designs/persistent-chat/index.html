Teammates: persistent named agents and group chat

Alex is a teammate with a permanent name, memory, and one durable chat. Agent sessions come and go underneath it. Group chats bring familiar teammates into one conversation. Use Create teammate, the Chats + menu, or an empty state's Create button to walk through setting one up. Add a picture in the form or in About; it stays in this tab's memory. Exploratory design only, not an approved implementation. Review notes are in REVIEW.md.
